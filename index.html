Research interests and projects

I'm particularly interested in macroeconomics, macro-finance and econometrics. Within macro, I'm most interested in business cycles, forecasting macroeconomic indicators, monetary economics and understanding how macroeconomic shocks spill over into financial markets.

Global Shocks and Indian Equity Returns: An Empirical Investigation of Information Transmission, Contagion, and Extreme Events (with Nikhil Rajan, LSE)

How much of a bad day in New York shows up the next morning in Mumbai? Using jump detection on S&P 500 and Nifty 50 returns, we estimate a transmission coefficient of 0.284 and find volatility running 3.42 times higher during crisis periods.

Stochastic Welfare Analysis: Impact of a 40% Tax on Cigarettes under the New GST Regime (with Nikhil Rajan and Sriram Rajagopalan, LSE)

Quantifies the net welfare gains of a steep sin tax using a three-state Markov chain and log-linear Cobb–Douglas utility with externalities.

Hamilton's Regime-Switching Models (with Sriram Rajagopalan, LSE)

A replication of Hamilton's (1989) Markov regime-switching filter and smoother, presented for stochastic processes coursework alongside Neftçi (1984) and Goldfeld and Quandt (1973).

Testing the New Economics of Labor Migration: Evidence from IHDS Panel Data

Tested the Stark and Bloom (1985) hypothesis for the Indian context using IHDS panel data.

Understanding the Shift from Agriculture to Non-Farm Employment in Rural India: A Socio-Economic Approach (with Riya Fathima and Dr. Aritri Chakravarty)

An empirical account of why rural Indian workers leave the farm, built on NSSO and IHDS household survey data and presented at the IIT Madras DOSS Conference.

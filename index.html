About

I'm an incoming master's student at the Barcelona School of Economics. My research interests include the study of macroeconomic shocks to financial markets, forecasting of business cycles and the how monetary policy affects both. In my undergrad, i have also worked on projects that explored the NSSO data and IHDS surveys.

In September 2026 I start the MSc Economics at the Barcelona School of Economics. The longer-term plan is a PhD in macrofinance and financial econometrics. A question I keep coming back to is how financial infrastructure gets used as an instrument of state power — sanctions, reserve currencies, weaponized interdependence — and whether the tools of financial econometrics can say something rigorous about it.

I work mainly in Stata and Python, write everything in LaTeX, and have spent more time than I'd like to admit checking for lookahead bias in backtests.

Research & Projects
Global Shocks and Indian Equity Returns: Information Transmission, Contagion, and Extreme Events
2025–26 · with Nikhil Rajan

How overnight global information moves the Nifty 50, 2019–2025. Baseline OLS, instrumented with US Treasury yields (2SLS), rolling jump detection across both indices, crisis interaction models, and a logit model of crash probability under stress.

Farm-to-Non-Farm Employment Transitions in Rural India
Ongoing · RA, Dr. Aritri Chakravarty

Research assistant and co-author on a paper studying employment transitions out of agriculture, using IHDS panel data and NSSO rounds. Work includes data construction, panel estimation, and drafting.

Rainfall Shocks and Agricultural Wage Income in Rural Karnataka
2025

IHDS panel matched with ICRISAT district rainfall data across 25 districts. Pooled OLS versus district fixed effects — the FE specification kills the naive rainfall effect, confirming upward bias in OLS from district-level confounders.

Paper (PDF)
Black–Scholes Implied Volatility Validation on Nifty 50 Options
2025

Pricing errors from Black–Scholes regressed on moneyness, time to expiry, and GARCH-estimated conditional volatility, to map where and why the model fails on Indian index options.

Mean-Reversion Backtesting Framework for NSE Equities
2026 · Python

A backtesting engine built to be hard to fool: anti-lookahead safeguards, realistic Indian delivery costs, out-of-sample splits, and Monte Carlo null testing. Main finding so far — timing alpha can be statistically real and still lose to buy-and-hold on CAGR and Sharpe, because time out of the market is expensive.
